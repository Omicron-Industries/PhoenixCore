# Source and Soul System

The **Source and Soul System** is a metaphysical layer of energy management that ties magical potency to the environment. It defines how **Source** is produced, consumed, and amplified based on the local **Soul Density** of the world.

---

## Soul Density
Every chunk in the world has an inherent **Soul Density** value. This value represents the "metaphysical health" of the land and acts as a multiplier for Source-based machines.

### Environmental Factors
- **Biomes**: Lush biomes like Forests and Groves have high base soul density (~1.5x), while magical or old-growth biomes can reach up to 2.5x. Deserts, wastelands, and corrupted lands have significantly lower density (0.1x - 0.5x).
- **Flora**: The presence of **Soul Flowers** and other magical vegetation provides a "Botanical Boost," further increasing the resonance in the area.
- **Natural Regeneration**: Chunks naturally recover their soul density over time if they have been depleted by heavy industrial or magical usage.

---

## Tools of the Trade: Soul Lens
The **Soul Lens** is the primary diagnostic tool for interacting with the soul field.

### Usage
- **Topography Map**: `Right-Click` to open a topographic map of the local soul field. This allows you to identify "hotspots" with high density for optimal machine placement.
- **Chunk Analysis**: View the exact density multiplier, maximum capacity, and current replenishment status of your current chunk.
- **Machine Interaction**: Like the Tesla Binder, it can be used on certain magical machines to configure their soul-frequency settings.

## Source Infrastructure

### 1. Alchemical Imbuer
The hub of your Source network. It acts as a link for natural soul resonance.
- **Harmonization**: Its efficiency is directly tied to the local Soul Density, botanical boosts, and harmonic bonuses from nearby structures.
- **Source Production**: Converts base materials into Source, with the output rate scaling based on the "Soul Resonance" of the area.

### 2. Bio-Aetheric Engine
A high-efficiency power generator that converts Source and biological resonance into EU.
- **Soul Saturation**: When placed in a high-density chunk, it can become "Soul Saturated," significantly increasing its energy output.
- **Botanical Synergy**: Gains bonuses from being surrounded by diverse magical flora.

### 3. Source Reactor
A late-game multiblock for high-pressure Source manipulation.
- **Pressure Logic**: The reactor's internal pressure and stability are influenced by the local Soul Density.
- **Operational Requirements**: The reactor will **NOT RUN** if the local soul density is below a certain threshold (typically 1.0x), requiring you to "fertilize" the area with Soul Flowers or move to a more vibrant biome.

---

## Recipe Conditions: Soul Resonance
Many high-tier magical and alchemical recipes require a minimum level of local **Soul Resonance** to be performed. 
- If the resonance is too low, the machine will pause or fail to start the recipe.
- Some specialized "Void" recipes may conversely require a very *low* soul resonance, forcing you to work in depleted or corrupted biomes.
