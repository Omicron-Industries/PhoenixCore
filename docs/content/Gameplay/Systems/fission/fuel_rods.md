# Fission Fuel Rods

Fuel rods are the primary energy source for your reactor. They determine the base heat production, energy output, and the duration of a fuel cycle.

---

## ☢️ Fuel Types

### Uranium-Based Rods (T1-T2)
- **Characteristics**: Low heat, moderate duration.
- **Best for**: Early-game power and initial isotope breeding.
- **Spectrum**: Provides a "Thermal" neutron spectrum.

### Plutonium-Based Rods (T3)
- **Characteristics**: High heat, high energy density.
- **Best for**: Mid-game industrial power.
- **Spectrum**: Provides a "Mixed Oxide" (MOX) spectrum.

### Exotic Fuels (T4-T5)
- **Characteristics**: Extreme heat, complex isotope outputs.
- **Best for**: Late-game OpV+ power and rare isotope harvesting.
- **Spectrum**: Provides a "Fast" neutron spectrum.

---

## 📊 Key Stats

- **Base Heat**: The raw HU/t generated per rod. This is multiplied by the number of rods and any moderator bonuses.
- **Neutron Flux**: High-tier rods generate a flux field that increases the efficiency of adjacent rods.
- **Fuel Cycle**: The duration (in ticks) a rod lasts before becoming "Spent."
- **Spent Fuel**: Every rod produces a specific spent variant that can be reprocessed into isotopes or new fuel.

---

## 💡 Pro Tips
- **Tier Consistency**: Reactors perform best when all fuel rods are of the same tier. Mixing tiers can cause thermal instability.
- **Reprocessing**: Never throw away spent rods. The isotopes extracted from T4+ spent fuel are essential for late-game progression.
