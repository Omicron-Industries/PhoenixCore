# Blanket Rods & Isotope Breeding

Blanket rods are specialized components used in **Breeder Reactors**. Unlike fuel rods, which generate power, blanket rods are designed to be irradiated by the reactor's neutron flux to transform into valuable isotopes.

---

## 🧬 Breeding Cycle

### 1. Irradiation
When placed in a Breeder Reactor, blanket rods (made of fertile materials like Thorium-232 or Uranium-238) absorb neutrons.
- **Consumption**: Blanket material is consumed slowly over time, similar to fuel rods.
- **Spectrum Sensitivity**: The speed and success of breeding depend on the "Neutron Spectrum" (Thermal, Mixed, or Fast) provided by your fuel rods.

### 2. Isotope Formation
As the cycle progresses, the blanket material "Transmutes" into new elements.
- **Weighted Rolls**: Every cycle completion has a chance to produce different isotopes based on the material's **Breeding Table**.
- **Instability**: Some rare isotopes are "Instable" and will cause temporary heat spikes in the reactor during formation.

---

## 📦 Key Blanket Materials

- **Thorium Blanket**: Used to breed Uranium-233. A stable, long-duration cycle.
- **Depleted Uranium Blanket**: Used to breed Plutonium-239. High energy potential but requires a "Fast" neutron spectrum for efficiency.
- **Exotic Blankets**: High-tier blankets used to harvest transuranic elements and rare isotopes like Californium or Americium.

---

## 💡 Breeding Tips
- **Maximize Flux**: Surround your blanket rods with high-tier fuel rods to increase the "Neutron Density" and speed up the breeding cycle.
- **Automation**: Breeding produces isotopes as outputs. Ensure your breeder multiblock has an **Output Bus** or is **Soul-Linked** to automatically extract these valuable products.
- **Isotope Selection**: Use specific moderators (like Beryllium) to "Shift" the neutron spectrum and favor the production of specific rare isotopes over common ones.
